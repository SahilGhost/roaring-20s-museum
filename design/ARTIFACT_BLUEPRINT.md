## Historical caution

The U.S. Senate rejected the Treaty of Versailles amid opposition to the League of Nations; the 1930 Hague agreement put the later Young Plan into effect. The classroom's Room 4 $400,000 total is explicitly a constructed math case. The source record supports Doheny's $100,000 transfer and Sinclair-related benefits, but it does not justify representing the puzzle total as an established cash-bribe figure. No claim that scandal caused Harding's fatal heart attack appears in the museum. The four Room 1 codes and six Room 3 file labels are invented puzzle markers.

## Media

File XE links to a Library of Congress digitized 1920 Harding recording. The Coolidge radio exhibit links to the Library of Congress guide to his original sound recordings and film. Those are archival media on source pages; the text-to-speech button is a separate browser voice. A student can record original narration later if the class rubric requires it.
